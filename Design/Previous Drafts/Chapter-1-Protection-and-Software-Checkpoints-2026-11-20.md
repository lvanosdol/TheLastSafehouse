# Chapter 1: Previous protection and software checkpoints

**Status:** Historical. The [current Chapter 1 design](../Chapter%201/Chapter-1-Design.md) replaces these checkpoints with hands-on protection and software-removal tasks.

5. **Protecting the device:** Introduce anti-malware, firewalls, and updates. Explain Windows Security, how to find it from Start or Settings, and the purpose of Virus & threat protection and Firewall & network protection. **Checkpoint:** Find Windows Security and identify the purpose of its protection areas in a simulated interface. No threat alert or threat response appears until the boss.
6. **Choosing and managing software:** Compare open-source and proprietary licenses, subscriptions and purchases, and the purpose of product keys. Discuss trusted sources, app stores, safe removal of unwanted or malicious software, and the risks of piracy. **Checkpoint:** Choose a trustworthy source and an appropriate way to manage a fictional application.
